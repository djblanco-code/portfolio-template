This is a sample post. It's here so you can see what a post looks like on your site before you write your own.

## How to write a post

Go to `/keystatic` on your site (for example, `https://your-site.vercel.app/keystatic`) and sign in with GitHub. Click **Posts**, then the **+** button. Give it a title, a one-line excerpt, and start writing.

When you click **Create** or **Save**, the editor saves your post into your GitHub repository. Vercel notices the change and rebuilds your site, and about a minute later the post is live.

## A few things you can use

- **Headings**, **bold**, *italics*, and [links](https://example.com)
- Lists like this one
- Images, uploaded straight from the editor

## Deleting this post

Open it in the editor and click the trash icon, or tick **Draft** to hide it without deleting it. Drafts stay saved but don't show on the site.
